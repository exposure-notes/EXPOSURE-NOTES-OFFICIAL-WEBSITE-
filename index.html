I capture moments, places, people and little details through my own perspective.

I don't use a dedicated camera. My phone is the tool I carry with me, and mobile photography is where I explore my creativity.

Exposure Notes is my visual collection of moments, moods and memories — captured, edited and kept as a part of the journey.
